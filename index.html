Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know before getting started.

What exactly do I get with a ClawConfig setup?
You get a fully configured OpenClaw instance tailored to your needs. That includes installation, messaging platform integration (WhatsApp, Telegram, Discord, etc.), skill configuration, heartbeat scheduling, memory search, workspace files (SOUL.md, AGENTS.md, etc.), and a walkthrough so you understand how everything works. The exact scope depends on your chosen package.
Do I need technical experience to use OpenClaw after setup?
Not at all. The whole point of ClawConfig is that we handle the technical parts. After setup, you interact with your agent through chat — just like texting a friend. We also include an orientation call to make sure you're comfortable with everything.
How long does the setup process take?
Most setups are completed within 24 hours of receiving access to your machine (via SSH or screen share). Complex CTO-tier configurations with custom automations may take up to 48 hours. You'll be kept in the loop throughout the entire process.
What platforms can you connect my agent to?
We support all platforms that OpenClaw integrates with: WhatsApp, Telegram, Discord, Signal, Slack, iMessage, and more. We also set up email monitoring, calendar integration, and browser automation depending on your package.
What if I'm not satisfied with the setup?
We offer a satisfaction guarantee. If something isn't working right during your support period, we'll fix it — no extra charge. If we fundamentally can't deliver what was promised, we'll work out a fair resolution. We're building a reputation here; your success is our success.
